Features that make Design simply awesome

Not sure if Design is for you? Check out a few of the key features!

Vectors

Use SVGs that always stay crisp and you can
change the color as you go.

Forms

Add forms to collect your visitor's emails
directly with Mailchimp.

Effects

Add some fancy elements to your designs by
adding interactive scroll effects or animations.

Paste CSS

Copy CSS from Photoshop or Sketch or even
your text editor and paste it.

Responsive from the start

Have full control over mobile layouts.Move the
breakpoints where it's best for the content.

Library

Choose from presets or create your collections
of elements to re-use later in your projects.
